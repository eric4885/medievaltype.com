Windows preview tip

On Chinese-locale Windows, Font Viewer often shows a Chinese sample sentence for any Latin-only TTF. That text is the OS sample string — not Chinese characters inside these fonts. Open the font in Word/Notepad, or read README.txt in the zip, to confirm Latin blackletter coverage.
