Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Summary

MedievalType is a static website. Text generators process your input in your browser only. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not upload the text you type into the tools.

What we do not collect through the tools

The gothic / medieval generators do not send your typed text to our servers. Copy and preview happen locally in the page.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may process standard request data (such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL) for security, caching and reliability. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently set first-party analytics cookies. If we add advertising or analytics later, this page will be updated before those scripts go live.

Font downloads

Downloading a font zip is a normal file request. Packs include the SIL OFL 1.1 license text. We do not use downloads to build marketing profiles.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on GitHub.
